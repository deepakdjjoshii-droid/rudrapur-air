How this is madeयह कैसे बनता है

Rudrapur has no official air monitor. These figures come from the Copernicus CAMS forecast for Rudrapur, corrected each day against nine official monitors: Govt Girls Inter College in Kashipur (50 km away, counted double because it is nearest), six in Moradabad and two in Bareilly. Over the past 10 days these monitors read 0.60× the forecast at their own locations, based on 1555 hourly readings, and Rudrapur's forecast is scaled by the same amount. Today's figure also uses the ratio measured so far today (0.60× over 0 readings), so a forecast spike the monitors do not see is toned down. Pooling several monitors keeps the correction working when one goes offline, which happens often in Kashipur.

Categories follow India's AQI for PM2.5 (CPCB). The hourly strip uses the same bands, as a guide. Fires are NASA VIIRS satellite detections.

Limits: the forecast grid is about 40 km wide, so a busy road or a fire next to you can be much worse. It misses sharp spikes such as festival fireworks: on Diwali 2026 the pollution board's own Rudrapur reading put PM2.5 at roughly twice this page's estimate. It also often overstates dust here. Treat these numbers as a good estimate for the town, not a street reading.

Data: Copernicus CAMS and weather via Open-Meteo · Kashipur monitor via OpenAQ · Fires: NASA FIRMS. Updated twice a day.
