How this is madeयह कैसे बनता है

Rudrapur has no official air monitor. These figures come from the Copernicus CAMS forecast for Rudrapur, corrected each day against the nearest official monitor, the Uttarakhand pollution board's station at Govt Girls Inter College, Kashipur (50 km away). This week the monitor read {{FACTOR}}× the forecast at Kashipur, based on {{FACTOR_HOURS}} matched hours, and Rudrapur's forecast is scaled by the same amount. Today's figure also uses the ratio measured so far today ({{NOW_FACTOR}}× over {{TODAY_HOURS}} hours), so a forecast spike the monitor does not see is toned down.

Categories follow India's AQI for PM2.5 (CPCB). The hourly strip uses the same bands, as a guide. Fires are NASA VIIRS satellite detections.

Limits: the forecast grid is about 40 km wide, so a busy road or a fire next to you can be much worse. It misses short spikes such as festival fireworks, and it often overstates dust here. Treat these numbers as a good estimate for the town, not a street reading.

Data: Copernicus CAMS and weather via Open-Meteo · Kashipur monitor via OpenAQ · Fires: NASA FIRMS. Updated twice a day.
